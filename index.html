ABOUT US

Welcome to Misty Valley Homestay, your peaceful escape nestled in the heart of Munnar’s mist-covered hills.


Surrounded by lush green tea plantations, rolling valleys, and fresh mountain air, our homestay offers a warm and homely stay experience for travelers seeking nature, comfort, and relaxation.

At Misty Valley Homestay, we believe that your journey should feel personal. Our rooms are designed to provide a cozy ambiance with modern amenities while keeping you connected to nature’s beauty — from the first light of sunrise to the cool evening breeze.


Whether you're here for a family vacation, a romantic getaway, or a quiet work-from-mountains retreat, we ensure:

  • Comfortable & well-maintained rooms
  • Friendly hospitality that makes you feel at home
  • Fresh home-cooked meals upon request
  • Scenic viewpoints just steps away

Come, slow down your pace and breathe in the misty magic of Munnar — where every stay becomes a memorable story.
